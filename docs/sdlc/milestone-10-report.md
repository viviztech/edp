## Delivered behavior

The organization reporting workspace contains 40 bilingual operational measures across marketing, program, learning, entrepreneurship, funding and support, plus impact coverage and measurement-edition aggregates. Every figure exposes its source, temporal basis, unit, definition, numerator/denominator and caveat. Dates are inclusive UTC days, limited to 366 days, with validated program/cohort filters. Current states are identified as current observations rather than historical states at the period end. Zero-denominator percentages are unavailable; missing financial transactions do not become an invented funding-facilitated amount. Source/campaign attribution remains organization-only when reliable cohort assignment is absent.

SQL aggregates count actual source facts without join fan-out or silently truncating participant populations. Funnels use actual lead/person/application/enrollment relationships. Registered people use their first immutable CRM conversion link, not an inferred signup date. Attendance excludes excused/missing marks from its explicitly recorded-mark denominator. Repeated business profiles, plan submissions and B02 outcomes cannot inflate current/latest measures; first ticket resolution is preserved after reopening. Membership-based funding/support scope is labeled as association, not causal attribution.

Managers author and publish bilingual numbered measurement editions. Participants independently record baseline/outcome business-started, business-active and jobs-created observations without a B02 profile. Identity and active/completed ownership are server-derived. Exact revision and request UUID guard append-only corrections, including a correction pinned to an archived edition. The reporting population retains scoped enrollments recorded by the end date, including withdrawal, while current eligibility restricts owner writes. Coverage counts distinct reported enrollments; same-edition matched samples alone produce jobs change. B02 data remains a separate source. No indicator policy, verified outcome or causal business-growth claim is fabricated.

Immutable snapshots preserve normalized filters, UTC generation time, dictionary version, referenced editions and complete validated evidence from one repeatable-read transaction. Lists return metadata only. Excessive edition groups or payloads produce explicit narrower-filter errors. Separate report view/export/manage grants protect the workflow: Directors/Super Admins can export, Managers can view/manage definitions, and other roles require explicit grants.

CSV exports are bounded durable jobs generated synchronously from an authorized frozen snapshot: at most 500 rows including header/context and 256 KiB. Metadata excludes CSV and capability values. The requester needs current view/export permissions and a reason, with idempotent request recovery. Jobs expire after 24 hours; five-minute hash-only capabilities reauthorize each download. Formula-like textual cells are escaped while signed numeric values remain numeric. Attachment/no-store/nosniff/no-referrer transport and raw-URL-log suppression protect capability paths. No raw participant lists, XLSX/PDF, worker/provider delivery or new dependency is introduced.
