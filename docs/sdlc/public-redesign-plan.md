# Public website redesign plan

Redesign the public EDP experience for people exploring entrepreneurship and practical business support. The page's job is to explain the opportunity and guide visitors to the right program information.

## Visual direction

- Palette: cloud `#F7F9FC`, ink `#142D4E`, cobalt `#2457E8`, mist `#E9EEFD`, mint `#DFF5EA`, sunshine `#FFDE82`.
- Typography: Sora for confident display headings, Hanken Grotesk for clear body/navigation text, Noto Sans Tamil for Tamil; self-hosted variable fonts.
- Layout: a floating compact header; large split hero; an illustrated learning/support grid; a selectable four-stage journey; helpful public-page links; FAQ; a strong closing invitation and full footer.
- Signature: a people-focused editorial photograph paired with a small idea-to-opportunity pathway card. It expresses community entrepreneurship rather than abstract technology.

The early concept of a growth chart and headline statistics was rejected: the program has no published impact numbers. The visual emphasis will be people, learning, and useful next steps. Preserve the existing logo and the honest program-availability message.

## Scope and acceptance

- Redesign the complete landing page, navigation, footer, and supporting public-page styles in both English and Tamil.
- Preserve published CMS content, routes, account access and existing program information.
- Add accessible mobile navigation, keyboard-operable dropdowns and a guided journey selector; respect reduced-motion settings.
- Keep the main page server-rendered and isolate interactive parts into small client components.
- Verify production build, lint/type checks, relevant existing tests, navigation interactions, links and image loading.
- Inspect screenshots at desktop and mobile widths in both languages and check for overflow/console errors.
- Publish the verified design to the existing Coolify staging app and record its deployment state.
