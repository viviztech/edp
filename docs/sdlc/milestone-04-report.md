## A02.1 selection configuration, rubric versioning and reviewer assignment

Added organization-scoped selection cycles bound one-to-one to application cycles. Each cycle starts with a versioned draft rubric containing ordered bilingual criteria, positive integer weights and bounded score ranges. Weights must total 100 before save/publication. Partial unique indexes allow at most one draft and one published rubric per cycle. Publication is separate from editing, retires the prior live version, and PostgreSQL triggers freeze published/retired rubrics and their criteria. Copying creates the next editable policy version without changing existing assignments.

Program Managers can configure cycles, rubrics and assignments. Program Directors and Super Admins additionally publish; Selection Committee members receive only selection.cycle.view and selection.review. Review activation requires the source application cycle to be closed and a rubric to be published. Assignment validates organization/intake/cycle/rubric/reviewer scope, pins the published rubric, and transitions the application from submitted to under_review with immutable history. The submitted application trigger now permits only this narrow state/version/timestamp update and continues to reject changes to identity, form, cycle, revision, application number, submitted timestamp and created timestamp. Assigned reviewers must declare clearance or a reasoned conflict before scoring; scoring remains unavailable for A02.2.

The English/Tamil selection workspace covers cycle creation, rubric editing/versioning/publication, activation, assignments, reviewer inbox and conflict declaration through a fixed route allowlist, session authorization, origin/CSRF checks, 64 KiB bodies, upstream timeouts and private no-store responses. Applicant-facing status accepts under_review without exposing assignments, rubric, conflicts or internal notes.

## A02.2 reviewer scoring, moderation and weighted aggregation

Reviewers can now save bounded criterion scores and an internal comment against their pinned rubric after declaring no conflict, then submit an immutable review. The server calculates a normalized weighted total out of 100 from the published criterion ranges and weights. Program Directors and Super Admins can exclude or restore a completed review with a mandatory reason; moderation changes inclusion only and preserves the submitted scores, comment, rubric and weighted total. Manager workspaces show counts and the mean of included completed reviews, while reviewer workspaces omit aggregate results.

## A02.3 interviews, decisions, overrides and finalization

Completed review evidence can now produce a reasoned shortlist. Program Managers, Directors and Super Admins can schedule an online or in-person interview with one to ten eligible panel members. Each assigned panel member submits one score from 0 through 100 and an internal comment; PostgreSQL locks that evidence after submission. Completion requires all panel members and stores the server-calculated mean. Directors and Super Admins can record selected, waitlisted or rejected decisions with review/interview score snapshots, then make append-only reasoned overrides between terminal states. Finalization is director-only and rejects cycles with unfinished assigned applications or open interviews. Applicant receipts expose the resulting application status without exposing staff comments or scores. Enrollment is intentionally pending P01.2 so participant creation and the unique selected-application enrollment transaction are introduced together.
